Refund policy

DIRECT ANSWER

Where do I request cancellation or a refund?

Use the original purchase channel: Paddle, Google Play, Apple’s App Store or an existing BMC membership. Rodinka’s operator can also help with service defects and statutory withdrawal. The rules below come from the shared Terms of Use; commercial refunds do not restrict statutory rights.

Pro availability, duration and renewal

New Pro purchases are being introduced gradually. Describing a sales channel here does not mean it has launched. A purchase is available only when the app displays a specific offer and the relevant channel allows you to confirm it. Without an available offer, these terms alone do not enable a purchase.

Where a subscription is offered, you choose a monthly or yearly period at the price shown before purchase. It repeats and charges automatically for the next period until renewal is turned off. Cancelling renewal keeps access until the end of the paid period; ordinary cancellation alone does not refund an already paid price. Withdrawal, defect claims and any refund are separate rights.

Uninstalling the app or deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Manage it in the original channel using the account that made the purchase. Rodinka support can help identify the channel; you do not need to buy Pro a second time.

Payments on the web through Paddle

If web purchases through Paddle.com are enabled, Paddle is the seller (Merchant of Record) for that order, rather than merely a payment gateway. It collects payment, issues receipts and handles applicable taxes and payment refunds. Rodinka’s operator remains the app provider. The order and receipt identify the relevant Paddle entity; Paddle’s buyer terms also apply.

Cancel an existing Paddle subscription through the customer portal at paddle.net or available subscription management in your Rodinka account. Request a refund from Paddle; you may also contact Rodinka support. An approved full refund of the relevant payment may end Pro if the family has no other active entitlement. Statutory rights take precedence over commercial refund policies.

Payments through Google Play

If the Android app offers Pro purchases, it uses Google Play Billing. Google Play handles ordering, billing and subscription management; Rodinka’s operator provides the service. The store terms, pre-purchase information and receipt identify the seller for the transaction. Depending on the country, Google may be the seller or an agent for the provider; we do not claim it always sells the service itself.

Cancel renewal in Google Play → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions, using the purchasing account. You may also use available subscription management in your Rodinka account. Follow Google Play’s process to request a refund; you may also contact Rodinka’s operator about service defects or statutory rights.

  • Cancel a Google Play subscription
  • Google Play refunds

Payments through the App Store

If the iOS app offers Pro purchases, it uses in-app purchases through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles billing and subscription management; Rodinka’s operator is the app provider. Apple’s terms, pre-purchase information and receipt determine the relevant Apple entity and its role in the transaction. The licence listed for the app in the App Store also applies to use of the iOS app.

Cancel renewal in device Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, using the purchasing account. You may also use available subscription management in your Rodinka account. Request a refund from Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com. Rodinka’s operator can also help with app defects and statutory rights.

  • Apple refunds

Previous Buy Me a Coffee subscriptions

Buy Me a Coffee (BMC) is a previous channel for existing subscribers only. Rodinka does not offer new BMC memberships. An existing subscription may continue and renew for its agreed period; BMC provides membership and payment infrastructure while Rodinka’s operator remains the service provider.

Manage an existing membership by signing in to your BMC account under Memberships or using the available management button in your Rodinka account on the web. Cancellation stops the next renewal and ordinarily preserves the paid period. Send a defect claim or refund request to the operator using the contacts below; payment processing also follows BMC’s rules. This is not an offer of a new purchase.

Statutory withdrawal and refunds

A consumer concluding a distance contract generally has 14 days from its conclusion to withdraw without giving a reason. Activating Pro or using the app does not automatically remove that right. Store commercial policies cannot restrict your statutory rights.

Early performance of an ongoing digital service may require your express request and proper information. A proportionate payment for service already supplied upon withdrawal is due only where the legal conditions are met. The exception for digital content supplied without a tangible medium requires, in particular, prior express consent, information and confirmation; these terms alone do not replace such consent or a request.

Send an unambiguous withdrawal statement to the operator’s email or postal address below, or to the seller identified on your receipt. Identify your account, order date and reference, and sales channel. Sending the statement within the time limit is sufficient. Rodinka support can help direct a payment request to the appropriate party. For valid statutory withdrawal, payments are refunded without undue delay and no later than 14 days after notice, using the same payment method unless otherwise agreed; any lawful proportionate service charge is unaffected.

Digital service defects and updates

You have statutory remedies for lack of conformity. The service must meet the contract and legal requirements for functionality, availability, security and necessary updates. For ongoing supply, we are responsible for defects arising or becoming apparent during the contractual period.

You may request a defect to be remedied free of charge within a reasonable time and without significant inconvenience. If remedy is impossible, unsuccessful, the defect recurs or is sufficiently serious, you may seek an appropriate price reduction or terminate under the applicable legal conditions. We do not exclude these rights through a blanket liability cap or an assertion that the service has no warranty.

How to make a defect claim

Send claims about Rodinka’s functionality or availability to privacy@mojerodinka.cz or the postal address below. Describe the defect, when it occurred and your requested remedy; your account email, app version and any order reference help us locate it. Do not send passwords or unnecessary health or family information.

We will confirm receipt and the substance of the claim, explain its handling and provide the outcome or reasons for rejection. Digital service claims will be resolved within a reasonable time considering the nature and purpose of the service, and we will keep you informed. Statutory remedies for delay or defects remain available. Direct payment claims also to the seller on your receipt; we can help identify the appropriate contact.

Alternative dispute resolution

If we cannot resolve a consumer dispute under a contract with the operator directly, for a paid service you may contact the Czech Trade Inspection Authority (Česká obchodní inspekce, ČOI), an alternative consumer dispute resolution body. Submit a proposal within one year of first asserting your claim with the trader. ADR seeks an agreement and does not restrict access to courts.

For a separate payment relationship with another seller, jurisdiction and procedure also depend on that seller’s terms and applicable law.
