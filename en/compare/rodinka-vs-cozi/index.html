Rodinka vs Cozi

Cozi is an established family organizer with a calendar, lists and a recipe box that can also show other calendars. Rodinka is a family planner in Czech, Slovak and English that covers children, trips, pets and memories as well as the calendar and shopping.

DIRECT ANSWER

What is the main difference between Rodinka and Cozi?

Cozi offers a shared calendar for up to 12 members, shopping lists, chores and a recipe box, and can show Google, Apple or Outlook calendars; the free version shows ads and, according to its FAQ, everyone sees and edits everything. Rodinka has adult and child roles, children’s accounts with rewards and pocket money, meal planning, trips and a family chronicle, no ads, and Czech and Slovak as well as English. It does not connect other calendars yet.

Who wrote this comparison

This comparison is written and published by Rodinka, one of the apps being compared. For the other apps we rely only on their official websites, and anything we could not verify is labelled as such. If you spot an inaccuracy, tell us and we will correct it. Last checked on 9 November 2026.

Which app suits which family

Rodinka suits you if…

  • you want an ad-free app, in Czech or Slovak if that matters to you,
  • you want children to see only what is meant for them and earn rewards for chores,
  • you also want trips, pets, documents and family memories in the same place.

Cozi suits you if…

  • you need work, school or sports calendars next to the family one,
  • you are happy in English and like an established app with a long history,
  • it is fine that everyone in the account has the same full access.

What it looks like in an ordinary week

A term of activities

In Cozi you add a recurring event and can bring a school calendar into the family view. In Rodinka you add the activity with a repeat, choose the child and who takes them, and change a single date without touching the series.

A child with their own phone

In Rodinka a child signs in without an email address and sees only their chores, the shopping and the calendar, not documents or family settings. According to Cozi’s FAQ, everyone in the account has full access to all data.

Shopping without signal

Both apps say the shopping list can be checked off offline. Rodinka also handles chores, the calendar and the Today screen offline.

Platforms and pricing

Rodinka

Rodinka is on the App Store and Google Play and runs as a full web app on computers; on Windows it is also in the Microsoft Store. Rodinka Free costs nothing, has no ads and needs no payment card. Rodinka Pro, for the whole family, adds more tools and photo storage; prices are not published yet.

Cozi

Cozi is available on the web, iPhone and Android. The free version is supported by ads. According to the FAQ, Cozi Gold costs USD 39 a year and covers everyone in the account; it adds no ads, a month view on mobile, search and more reminders, among other things. The two-week trial applies only to subscriptions started on the web. A higher Max plan adds AI features.

Frequently asked questions

Is Rodinka a Cozi alternative?

Yes, if you want an ad-free family planner with children’s accounts, especially in Czech or Slovak. If seeing work or school calendars inside the family calendar is essential, Cozi does that today and Rodinka does not yet.

Is Cozi available in Czech?

We could not verify Czech or Slovak on Cozi’s official website. Rodinka’s whole interface is in Czech, Slovak and English.

Can I limit what children see in Rodinka?

Yes. A child account does not see documents or family settings, and a helper such as a grandparent sees only the sessions you pick them for. According to its FAQ, Cozi does not offer restrictions like this.

How much does Cozi Gold cost?

According to Cozi’s FAQ, USD 39 a year for the whole account (checked on 9 November 2026). Rodinka Free costs nothing; Rodinka Pro prices are not published yet.

Can I add a photo to a memory or an event?

In Rodinka, yes: memories and milestones carry photos in the shared chronicle. According to Cozi’s FAQ, it does not currently support attaching images or files to events or lists.

Which one to choose

If your family works in English and you need work and school calendars next to the family plan, Cozi is a proven choice.

If you want to involve children with their own account, keep the app free of ads and have trips, pets and family memories in it too, try Rodinka.
