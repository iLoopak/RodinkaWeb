Rodinka vs Familia

Both apps want to make family planning lighter. Familia is built around capturing tasks quickly with AI help; Rodinka around one shared overview of the whole household, including children, meals, trips and memories.

Start for free →Features side by side ↓

DIRECT ANSWER

What is the main difference between Rodinka and Familia?

Familia is built for quick capture: voice, text or a photo becomes a draft task or plan that you simply confirm, and it connects to Google Calendar. Rodinka covers more of family life: a calendar that shows who is taking whom, children’s accounts with rewards and pocket money, meal planning, shopping, trips, pets and memories, and it works in a browser and offline. It does not connect to other calendars yet.

Who wrote this comparison

This comparison is written and published by Rodinka, one of the apps being compared. For the other apps we rely only on their official websites, and anything we could not verify is labelled as such. If you spot an inaccuracy, tell us and we will correct it. Last checked on 9 November 2026.

Which app suits which family

Rodinka suits you if…

  • you want the calendar, chores, shopping, meals and trips in one family space,
  • children should take part with their own sign-in and rewards or pocket money,
  • you want to use it in a browser on a computer without a paid plan.

Familia suits you if…

  • you add a lot on the go and like to dictate or photograph a note,
  • you want tasks and plans connected to Google Calendar,
  • you mainly use a phone app on iPhone or Android.

What it looks like in an ordinary week

A note from nursery

In Familia you photograph the note and get a draft to review. In Rodinka you type the date yourself and say straight away who it is for and who will take the child.

A week of cooking

In Rodinka you plan the week’s meals, the family can vote and missing ingredients move to the shopping list in one step. We did not find meal planning on Familia’s website.

Chores for children

In Rodinka a child has their own sign-in, ticks off a chore and, once an adult confirms it, gets the reward or pocket money. For Familia we could not verify children’s profiles or rewards.

Frequently asked questions

Is Rodinka an alternative to Familia?

Yes, if you want a family planner that also covers children, meals, trips and memories. If AI-assisted capture and a Google Calendar connection matter most, Familia will suit you better.

Do both apps support Czech?

Yes. Rodinka’s whole interface is in Czech, Slovak and English. Familia says on its website that it supports Czech; we could not verify Slovak.

Do I have to pay to use it on a computer?

Not with Rodinka: the web version is the main way to use it and is part of Rodinka Free. According to Familia’s pricing, its web version is part of the Smart plan.

Can I see my Google Calendar in Rodinka?

Not yet. Rodinka does not connect external calendars at the moment; you add family dates directly. Familia does offer a Google Calendar connection.

Do children get their own account?

In Rodinka, yes: a child signs in without an email address and sees only their own chores, the shopping list and the calendar. We did not find information about children’s accounts on Familia’s website.

Which one to choose

If you mainly want to get things out of your head and into an app as fast as possible, and your calendar lives in Google, try Familia.

If you want the whole family, children and grandparents included, to see the same plan and handle shopping, meals and trips in it too, try Rodinka. Both have a free version, so you can try them side by side.
