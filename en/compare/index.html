DIRECT ANSWER

How do you choose between Rodinka, Familia, FamilyWall and Cozi?

It mostly comes down to what your family deals with most often. Rodinka focuses on everyday family life in Czech, Slovak and English: a calendar that shows who is taking whom, chores with rewards for children, shopping, meals, trips and memories. Familia is built around quick AI-assisted capture, FamilyWall adds location sharing, and Cozi connects to other calendars. Every comparison lists its sources and when they were checked.

How we compare

Official sources only

For the other apps we use their own websites and help centers, not reviews or guesses. Every table row links to a source.

Unverified is not missing

When we cannot find something, we say “Could not verify”. We never treat silence on a website as a missing feature.

Rodinka as it is

For Rodinka we list only what the app does today, including what it does not do yet.

No scores

We do not hand out ratings or stars. We describe the differences and leave the decision to you.

Who wrote this comparison

This comparison is written and published by Rodinka, one of the apps being compared. For the other apps we rely only on their official websites, and anything we could not verify is labelled as such. If you spot an inaccuracy, tell us and we will correct it. Last checked on 9 November 2026.
