Rodinka vs FamilyWall

FamilyWall is a family app with a calendar, lists, a messenger, a gallery and location sharing. Rodinka focuses on everyday household planning in Czech, Slovak and English, with children’s accounts, meals, trips and memories.

DIRECT ANSWER

What is the main difference between Rodinka and FamilyWall?

FamilyWall combines a shared calendar and lists with a family messenger, a photo gallery and location sharing; the meal planner, documents and the Google and Outlook calendar connection are in Premium. Rodinka offers a calendar that shows who is taking whom, chores with rewards and pocket money for children, meal planning, trips, pets and a shared chronicle in its free version, with the whole interface in Czech, Slovak and English. It does not share locations and does not connect other calendars yet.

This comparison is written and published by Rodinka, one of the apps being compared. For the other apps we rely only on their official websites, and anything we could not verify is labelled as such. If you spot an inaccuracy, tell us and we will correct it. Last checked on 9 November 2026.

Which app suits which family

Rodinka suits you if…

  • you want family planning in Czech or Slovak, including for grandparents,
  • you want to give children their own account with chores, rewards and pocket money,
  • you plan meals and trips and do not want a paid plan for that.

FamilyWall suits you if…

  • you want to share locations in the family and get arrival alerts for home or school,
  • your family chats and shares photos and videos in one app,
  • you need your Google or Outlook work calendar next to the family one.

What it looks like in an ordinary week

Who picks up from practice

In Rodinka the practice shows who it is for and who is taking her, and Grandma can confirm by link without an account. In FamilyWall the date is in the shared calendar and, according to the website, you can see where everyone is.

A week of meals

In Rodinka the meal plan, with voting and ingredients sent to the shopping list, is part of the free version. In FamilyWall the meal planner with recipes is part of Premium, according to its help center.

Passports before a holiday

Rodinka reminds you well before documents expire, and only adults can see them. FamilyWall offers document sharing in Premium; we did not find expiry reminders on its website.

Platforms and pricing

Rodinka

Rodinka runs in the browser on phones, tablets and computers and can be added to the home screen like an app; on Windows it is also in the Microsoft Store. Rodinka Free costs nothing, needs no payment card and includes the calendar, chores, shopping, meals, trips, memories and documents. Rodinka Pro, for the whole family, adds more tools and photo storage; prices are not published yet.

FamilyWall

FamilyWall is available on the web, iPhone, iPad and Android. The core is free; Premium covers the whole family, can be paid monthly or yearly and starts with a 30-day trial. You can subscribe through Google Play, the App Store or the website. The site shows the price with a $ sign and no currency code, so we do not quote it.

Frequently asked questions

Is Rodinka a good FamilyWall alternative?

If you want a family planner with children’s accounts, meals and trips in the free version, and Czech or Slovak matters, yes. If you need location sharing or a family messenger with video, Rodinka does not replace that.

Does Rodinka share locations like FamilyWall?

No. Rodinka does not share family members’ locations. Family places are there for planning and opening directions to where something happens.

Is FamilyWall available in Czech?

We could not verify Czech or Slovak on FamilyWall’s official website. Its help center is in English, French, German, Portuguese and Spanish.

How do I move my calendar from FamilyWall to Rodinka?

Rodinka does not offer automatic import yet. Recurring dates such as activities only need to be added once with a repeat, and one-off events as they come.

Which FamilyWall features are paid?

According to FamilyWall’s help center, these include the meal planner, document sharing, the budget tracker, timetables, Google and Outlook calendar connection, enhanced location and extra storage.

Which one to choose

If seeing where family members are matters to you and you want to chat and share photos in the same app, FamilyWall is a good choice.

If you mainly want to plan the week together, involve children with chores of their own, and have meals and trips without a paid plan, try Rodinka.
