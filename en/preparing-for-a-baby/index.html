How Rodinka helps you prepare for a baby

Expected Child is an organizing space inside Rodinka, not a health or pregnancy-tracking app. It offers light orientation and a practical place to prepare together.

  1. 1

    A week-by-week journey

    An approximate week, simple progress and selected moments help place preparation in time. This is not medical measurement, monitoring or health advice.

  2. 2

    Preparation by topic

    Browse optional ideas for travel, sleep, feeding and breastfeeding, hygiene and changing, clothing, home, hospital and the first days, or administration. A checklist item can also become an ordinary chore, shopping item or event, so preparation does not stay in a list of its own.

  3. 3

    Names you consider together

    Collect possible names, mark favorites and share preferences such as like, maybe or probably not. It is a conversation aid, not a contest or score.

Common questions about preparing for a baby

Is Rodinka a pregnancy tracker?

No. Rodinka does not replace a medical app, professional guidance or advice from your doctor. It helps a family organize practical preparation with a light, approximate journey.

Do we have to complete the whole checklist?

No. It is optional inspiration and an overview, not a required shopping plan. Every family can keep only what matches its own needs.

Can we choose a name together?

Yes. You can collect possible names, mark favorites and capture each participating adult’s preference without turning the choice into a leaderboard.

Will children automatically see this information?

No. Expected Child is for the adults in the family, so preparation and sensitive information stay in their shared adult space.
