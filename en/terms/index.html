What do Rodinka’s terms cover?

The operator, Free and Pro, conditional channel availability, withdrawal, defect claims and consumer rights. The authoritative text is in the Rodinka app.

The service and its operator

Rodinka is provided by Lukáš Sitta, a Czech sole trader. Full identification and contact details appear at the end of this page. These terms cover the web and native apps.

Rodinka is an ongoing digital service for family organisation: a shared calendar, tasks, shopping, planning, private messages and family records. The family space is not a public social network. Health features support planning and do not provide healthcare.

You need a supported current browser or a compatible Android or iOS app, with internet access for your account and synchronisation. Calendar, tasks and shopping have limited offline support; purchases, subscription verification and some other features require a connection. Install available updates needed for the security and functionality of the service.

Accounts and contract formation

Creating an account or accepting an invitation requests access to Rodinka from its operator; the relationship for the free service starts when account access is provided. An adult family member creates child profiles and managed child accounts. An adult orders paid Pro.

Registration, an invitation or viewing this page does not order paid Pro. A paid subscription requires a separate order confirmation in an available sales channel and acceptance under that channel’s rules. The product, total price including applicable taxes, period and renewal rules must be provided before confirmation; that channel supplies the receipt and confirmation.

Rodinka Free and Rodinka Pro

Free provides the basic family organiser at no charge with 200 MiB for family photos. Pro increases storage to 5 GiB and adds Moment of the Day, personalisation and other features marked Pro, such as the family yearbook, Family Week and creating scenarios and preparations. Pro covers a specific family; another family under the same account does not automatically receive it.

Family records and photos are not automatically deleted just because Pro ends. The Free limit applies, so new photos cannot be added above it; new Moments of the Day and features requiring active Pro become unavailable. Existing preparations remain readable, editable and completable. Already saved yearbook PDFs are unaffected. Account or family deletion has separate rules below and in the Privacy Policy.

Cancelling renewal keeps access until the end of the paid period; ordinary cancellation alone does not refund an already paid price. Withdrawal, defect claims and any refund are separate rights.

Uninstalling the app or deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Manage it in the original channel using the account that made the purchase. Rodinka support can help identify the channel; you do not need to buy Pro a second time.

Statutory withdrawal and refunds

A consumer concluding a distance contract generally has 14 days from its conclusion to withdraw without giving a reason. Activating Pro or using the app does not automatically remove that right. Store commercial policies cannot restrict your statutory rights.

Early performance of an ongoing digital service may require your express request and proper information. A proportionate payment for service already supplied upon withdrawal is due only where the legal conditions are met. The exception for digital content supplied without a tangible medium requires, in particular, prior express consent, information and confirmation; these terms alone do not replace such consent or a request.

Send an unambiguous withdrawal statement to the operator’s email or postal address below, or to the seller identified on your receipt. Identify your account, order date and reference, and sales channel. Sending the statement within the time limit is sufficient. Rodinka support can help direct a payment request to the appropriate party. For valid statutory withdrawal, payments are refunded without undue delay and no later than 14 days after notice, using the same payment method unless otherwise agreed; any lawful proportionate service charge is unaffected.

What does not belong in Rodinka

Content you put into the app — messages, photos, documents and record titles — must not:

  • break the law,
  • endanger or exploit children in any way,
  • contain threats, harassment or intimidation,
  • contain hateful content directed at a group of people,
  • share material you do not have the rights to.

Reporting content

Every message has a "Report message" option in its menu. Reports go to the app operator, who reviews the content and removes or disables it if it breaks these guidelines.

Reports marked as child safety are handled with priority.

You can only report a message from a conversation you are part of.

Content removal

The operator may remove content that breaks these guidelines. The message text is no longer available in the app and the app stops providing access to its attachments; the underlying stored files are subsequently removed as part of moderation.

You can always delete your own messages yourself.
