Terms of use and content guidelines

These terms describe how to use Rodinka, what does not belong in it and how the Rodinka Pro subscription works.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

DIRECT ANSWER

What do Rodinka’s terms cover?

Who operates Rodinka, how Rodinka Free and the Rodinka Pro subscription work, that web payments are handled by Paddle.com as merchant of record, how to cancel and request a refund, and what content does not belong in Rodinka.

The service and its operator

Rodinka is operated by Lukáš Sitta. These terms apply to using Rodinka on the web and in the apps. The operator’s contact details are at the end of this page.

Rodinka is for private communication inside a single family. Conversations are not public and you cannot contact strangers through them.

Rodinka Free and Rodinka Pro

Rodinka can be used for free. Rodinka Pro is an optional subscription for the whole family: it adds 5 GB for family photos, Moment of the Day and personalisation. Current prices and the difference between the plans are on the pricing page at mojerodinka.cz/en/pricing.

When Rodinka Pro ends, family data is not deleted; you just cannot add new photos beyond the Free limit or new Moments of the Day.

Payments on the web through Paddle

Our order process on the web is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the merchant of record for all our orders. Paddle takes the payment, issues the receipt, handles tax and answers payment and refund enquiries. Paddle’s buyer terms also apply to your order.

A subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play follows that store’s rules.

Renewal, cancellation and refunds

Your subscription renews automatically for another month or year until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time at paddle.net or in the app under More → Account and family → Manage subscription. Rodinka Pro then stays active until the end of the paid period. Deleting your account does not cancel the subscription by itself.

You request refunds from Paddle at paddle.net under the Refund policy at mojerodinka.cz/en/refund-policy and the Paddle Refund Policy. An approved full refund of a payment ends Rodinka Pro. Your statutory consumer rights are not affected.

What does not belong in Rodinka

Content you put into the app — messages, photos, documents and record titles — must not:

  • break the law,
  • endanger or exploit children in any way,
  • contain threats, harassment or intimidation,
  • contain hateful content directed at a group of people,
  • share material you do not have the rights to.

Reporting content

Every message has a "Report message" option in its menu. Reports go to the app operator, who reviews the content and removes or disables it if it breaks these guidelines.

Reports marked as child safety are handled with priority.

You can only report a message from a conversation you are part of.

Content removal

The operator may remove content that breaks these guidelines. The message text is no longer available in the app and the app stops providing access to its attachments; the underlying stored files are subsequently removed as part of moderation.

You can always delete your own messages yourself.
