Help from family still gets arranged by phone

You share a calendar with your partner, but Grandma hears about a change on the phone and the nanny from a message that gets buried. By the time a parent leaves work, they often do not know whether the pick-up is really on.

Giving grandparents the whole family calendar is not the answer either. They do not want to scroll through shopping, chores and messages, and you do not want to share everything. They need to see their own sessions and say whether they can make it.

How Rodinka brings in the wider family

Four options you can use on their own. None of them gives anyone access to your whole family unless you invite them in as a member.

  1. 1

    Companion confirmation by link

    For a session where someone outside the family takes the children, create a link and send it however you usually message. They see the title, time, place, your notes and the children’s names if you allow it. They answer “Yes, I’ll go” or “I can’t”, with a short note if they like, and you see the answer on the session.

  2. 2

    Helpers with their own sign-in

    Someone who helps regularly can have their own account. You invite them by email, and in Rodinka they see only the sessions you pick them for as the companion, confirming each one. You can remove their access at any time.

  3. 3

    One account in several families

    An account can belong to more than one family — your own household and your parents’ family, say. Each keeps its own information and settings, and you switch between them in the app header.

  4. 4

    Handing a profile to a child

    When a child starts using their own email and password, you hand their profile over. Memories, milestones, chores and pocket money stay, and the password you knew stops working. The profile remains a child profile with the same rules as before.

Questions about the wider family and helpers

Does Grandma need a Rodinka account?

No. A link is enough to confirm one session, and it opens on her phone without signing in. If she helps regularly, she can create her own account and see all her sessions together.

Can someone outside our family help with the children?

Yes. Save a nanny, a neighbour or a friend’s parent among your other people and pick them as the companion. They do not become a member of your family: they get either a link or helper access to their own sessions.

What does a helper see?

Only the sessions you pick them for as the companion: the title, date and time, place, the names of the children they accompany and whether it is confirmed. They never see the family calendar, chores, shopping, messages, notes or documents. When you remove their access, the sessions disappear for them straight away.

Who can see a companion invitation link?

Anyone you send it to, so send it only to the person it is for. The page shows the title, date and time, the place with its address, both your names and your notes; the children’s names only if you allow it. The link stays valid until a week after the session at most, and you can cancel it at any time.

Can I belong to more than one family?

Yes. One account can belong to several families. In one you might be a parent, in another a child or another adult. The families’ information never mixes, and you switch between them in the header. For now, notifications on one device come from the family you turned them on for.

What happens when a child starts using their own account?

You hand the profile over with a link that works only with their confirmed email address. The whole history stays with the same profile, and the managed child sign-in you looked after stops working. The profile does not become an adult one: the family’s rules stay as they were and parents see what they saw before. Rodinka does not yet have a more independent mode for grown-up children.

Is this part of Rodinka Pro?

No. Companion confirmation, helpers, several families and handing over a profile all work without Rodinka Pro.
