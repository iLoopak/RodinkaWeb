Privacy policy

Rodinka is a family organiser. This document describes what the app actually stores, why, and who can see it.

Current document in the Rodinka app

Last updated: 11 November 2026

DIRECT ANSWER

How does Rodinka handle personal data?

Rodinka stores only what the family enters and the technical data needed to run the service. Analytics is off by default, you enter payment details directly with Paddle, and you can delete your account yourself in the app at any time.

Who controls your data

The controller of personal data and operator of Rodinka is Lukáš Sitta. For privacy questions and requests, email privacy@mojerodinka.cz.

What we store

Rodinka stores data you enter or create, together with technical data needed to run the account securely and provide its features:

  • Account: email address and authentication credentials handled by Supabase Auth, or Google sign-in if you choose it. Family and profile tables do not store a readable password.
  • Family: household membership, roles (admin, parent, child), invitations.
  • Profiles and photos: name, colour, optionally a profile photo and a family photo.
  • Calendar, tasks and activities: events, their time, place, participants and assignments.
  • Health planning: medical appointments and vaccinations — date, type, who they concern, who accompanies, and any note.
  • Documents: family document records, their expiry dates and optional attachments.
  • Places: family places including addresses and coordinates.
  • Safe arrival: which saved family place you confirmed arriving at, and when — or, only if you choose it, a one-time snapshot of your device’s precise location (latitude, longitude, an accuracy estimate and an optional readable place label); and, if you set one, the family place and time by which you mean to confirm your arrival.
  • Meal plans and the shopping list.
  • Messages: message text, reactions and attachments within your family.
  • Notes about family members: text, an optional title and optional details (a name and a value) that an adult member writes about themselves or another family member, and whether the note is private or shared with the adults in the family.
  • Notifications: your browser or device push endpoint and your reminder settings.

Why we process data

We use the following legal bases according to the purpose:

  • Core account and family-organiser functions — providing the service the user requested.
  • Security, abuse prevention and necessary operational protection — legitimate interest in running a secure and reliable service.
  • Optional analytics — consent.
  • Health information — express consent.
  • Support, privacy correspondence and requests — responding to the user request and legitimate operational interest.

Health information

Health planning details are stored only after you give express consent. Without it the app will not store a health record — the check is enforced by the database, not only by the interface.

You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings. Withdrawing blocks future storing and editing, but does not automatically erase records already stored. They remain readable until you delete them or request deletion at privacy@mojerodinka.cz.

This is not medical documentation and not the provision of healthcare services.

If you choose to write health information into a note about a family member, we store it as part of that note and use it only to show the note to you and to the adults you share it with. Rodinka does not evaluate the content of notes, and notes are not medical documentation.

Shared family space and access

Rodinka is a shared family space. Information you enter about yourself or other members may be visible to other family members according to their role, the visibility setting and the record type. Children’s accounts cannot see health records by default.

Access is enforced by the database at row level and derived from the signed-in user, never from values sent by the app.

Notes about family members: only the author can see a private note; every adult member of the family can read a shared one. Only the author can edit or delete it. Children’s accounts cannot see notes. The content of notes is never sent to analytics, operational diagnostics or notifications.

Safe arrival and one-time location sharing

In Safe arrival, a family member can confirm by hand that they have arrived at one of the family’s saved places. This way of confirming does not use the device’s location.

If you instead choose “Share my current location once” and confirm reading it, the app reads your device’s precise location once and stores the latitude, longitude and an accuracy estimate. The app requests location permission only at that moment. This is not ongoing tracking: Rodinka does not read your location in the background or repeatedly, does not use geofencing and keeps no location or route history.

The current confirmation or location is visible to the member themselves and to the active adult members of the same family. Other children in the family cannot see it, and other families have no access to it.

To show a readable place label, the coordinates are passed through our server to LocationIQ (see Processors) right after they are read, before you share them with your family.

Only the current state is kept: each new confirmation replaces the previous one, and cancelling it deletes it. A shared location expires after 2 hours at most and at the end of the family’s day at the latest; a saved-place confirmation after 8 hours at most and at the end of the day. Once expired it is no longer shown to anyone, and it is removed from the database the next time the feature is used in the family.

If you switch on “Check in when you arrive” yourself, the chosen family place and the time by which you mean to confirm your arrival are stored. At that time the app reminds you to confirm; if no confirmation comes within 15 minutes, the family’s active adult members get a generic notification that it has not come yet. This does not use your device’s location. At most one such expectation is kept per member: confirming your arrival at the same place or cancelling deletes it, a new one replaces the previous one, and it expires 3 hours after the chosen time at the latest.

Without an internet connection the location cannot be shared; the app neither stores it on the device nor sends it later.

Notifications

Lock-screen notifications never contain message content, sender names, health details or document names. Only generic wording is shown; the details appear once you open the app.

Safe arrival notifications carry only generic wording (“Someone in your family …”) — never a name, place, time, address or coordinates. Who and where is shown only once you open the signed-in app.

Analytics

Analytics is off by default. Until you turn it on, the analytics tool is not even downloaded and no usage data is sent.

Health data, message content, documents and addresses are never sent to analytics.

Safe arrival never sends precise coordinates, addresses, the exact accuracy of a location, names or places to analytics — only general categories, such as whether a location could be read.

Operational diagnostics

When error monitoring is enabled for a deployment, Sentry receives a technical record of an unexpected error to improve stability and support operational diagnostics: error type, sanitized stack, application release, platform, connectivity state and sanitized screen path.

Rodinka deliberately excludes names, email addresses, user, member and family identifiers, and the contents of family records from these reports. It removes custom error messages, URL queries and fragments, and other unnecessary context. The provider may see an IP address during network transport.

Operational diagnostics are technically separate from PostHog product analytics and its consent switch.

Smart Quick Add with AI (upcoming feature)

We are preparing an optional feature, Smart Quick Add: from a short text an adult family member types or dictates, it suggests shopping list items and tasks. The feature is not publicly available yet, and Rodinka does not process any family data for it so far. We will update this section before we make the feature available.

Once available, the text you enter into the feature is sent through our server to Mistral AI SAS (France), which prepares a suggestion from it. The text may contain names of family members, including children, and anything else you write in it. We do not send your family member list or profiles to Mistral AI; names in a suggestion are matched to members only by our server. Before the first use, the app will ask for your explicit permission.

The AI only suggests: nothing is written to the calendar, tasks or shopping list until you confirm the suggestion yourself. Child accounts cannot use the feature.

Rodinka does not store the text you enter or the suggestion in its usage database. It keeps only technical usage records linked to your account and family (for example the time, number of suggested items, outcome and consumption) to enforce limits and costs; after 13 months they are replaced by anonymous monthly totals only.

The purpose is to make adding items and tasks easier; the legal basis is performance of the contract for using Rodinka, for a feature you start yourself. Mistral AI will process the text as our processor under a data processing agreement, with hosting in the EU, and before launch we will switch off the use of this data for model training. Under its terms the provider may keep inputs and outputs for a limited time, for example to protect against abuse, and parts of its infrastructure (for example a content delivery network and security services) may operate outside the EEA under contractual safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. We will verify and describe the exact retention terms before launch. The rights described in Your rights and contact apply.

Processors

The following services are involved in running Rodinka:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage.
  • Vercel — application hosting.
  • Resend — sending sign-in and password reset emails.
  • Proton Mail — support and privacy correspondence, including data-subject requests.
  • PostHog — analytics, only if you enable it.
  • Sentry — technical records of unexpected errors when operational diagnostics are enabled for the deployment.
  • LocationIQ — address suggestions when searching for a place, and turning the coordinates read for a one-time Safe arrival location share into a readable place or address label (reverse geocoding); the coordinates are passed through our server.
  • Google — only if you choose Google sign-in.
  • Mapy.com, OpenStreetMap, Apple Maps, Google Maps — opened as an external link when you ask for directions.
  • Browser and device push services — notification delivery; the payload is encrypted.
  • Mistral AI SAS (France) — the upcoming optional Smart Quick Add feature; no family data is sent to it until the feature is available.

Web payments and Paddle

If Pro web purchases through Paddle.com are available, you provide payment and billing details to Paddle as seller (Merchant of Record), under its privacy policy; Rodinka does not store your payment card details.

For verification, Rodinka processes customer, transaction and subscription identifiers, payment and refund status, and the paid period. Describing the integration does not mean purchases have launched.

Payments through Google Play

If Android purchases are available, Google Play processes payment details under its privacy policy. Rodinka does not store payment card details.

For verification, Rodinka receives purchase and product identifiers, subscription status and validity. Purchase availability depends on the specific offer in the app.

Payments through the App Store

If iOS purchases are available, Apple processes payment details under its privacy policy. Rodinka does not store payment card details.

For verification, Rodinka receives transaction and product identifiers, subscription status, automatic renewal and validity. Purchase availability depends on the specific offer in the app.

Previous BMC memberships

For existing Buy Me a Coffee subscribers, Rodinka processes membership identification, payment events, the paid period and the email needed for previous matching. BMC processes payments under its own policies. Rodinka does not offer new memberships through this channel.

International processing

Rodinka does not claim that all processing remains only in the European Union or European Economic Area. Depending on provider infrastructure and deployment settings, some providers may process data in other countries. The exact locations and any transfer mechanisms depend on the relevant provider configuration and terms.

How long we keep data

Ordinary user content is kept while Rodinka is used, until the item is deleted or you delete your account.

Some consent, security, moderation and audit records may remain longer where necessary to document consent, protect the service or handle a security or moderation incident. Provider-side technical copies and operational logs follow provider settings; Rodinka does not promise an unverified fixed period for them.

Safe arrival keeps only a short-lived current state and no history — see Safe arrival and one-time location sharing above.

Notes about family members are kept until the author deletes them, or until the author, the member the note is about or the whole family is permanently removed. Notes written by or about a member who has been removed from the family are hidden and kept in case the membership is restored.

Storage and security

All communication uses HTTPS. Push notification payloads are encrypted.

For offline use the app keeps a copy of your calendar, tasks and shopping list on your device. Document attachments are never stored on the device.

Signing out removes the local copy and any cached photos from that device.

Your rights and contact

In the app you can at any time: turn analytics on or off, adjust or disable notifications, withdraw health data consent, and delete individual records you created.

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, object to processing, receive a copy or portability of your data, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Send requests to privacy@mojerodinka.cz.

You can delete your account yourself in the app: More → Account and family → Delete account. Deletion happens as soon as you confirm and signs you out on every device; copies in backups disappear within 30 days. It deletes the account, your family profile, profile photo, settings, notifications, private notes, wishes and your messages in private conversations, including their photos. Shared family records (calendar, tasks, shopping, memories and their photos, children’s records, the family group chat) stay with the other members, without your name. If you are the last adult in the family, the whole family is deleted, including the children’s profiles and their sign-ins. A child’s account deletes only its own sign-in; the child’s profile stays with the parents. Only a record that the deletion took place is kept (the account identifier and time, without your name or e-mail). A subscription paid through the App Store or Google Play is not cancelled by deleting your account — cancel it in the store’s settings.

Rodinka does not offer a bulk data export inside the app; requests for a copy of your data are handled manually.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Czech supervisory authority, the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, ÚOOÚ).
