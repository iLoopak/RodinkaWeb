Free and Pro compared

Calendar, chores, shopping, meals, trips, memories, documents and the extended family are all in Rodinka Free, and Rodinka Pro does not limit them. Pro adds helpers for the adults: the Family week, an overview of what still needs sorting that week; Family scenarios and the preparations you make from them for trips and events; suggested items based on your recent shopping; a suggested meal plan from your own meal library; and the Family yearbook as a print-ready PDF. On top of that come 5 GiB for photos instead of 200 MiB, Moment of the Day, and the option to personalise colours and the main navigation.

Shopping and meal suggestions come only from your own family data, and nothing is saved until you confirm it. In Rodinka Free you can add the same things by hand.

Rodinka Pro belongs to the whole family. One adult subscribes and every member of that family gets the benefits.

When Rodinka Pro ends, nothing is deleted. Photos and saved Moments of the Day stay, and you can still view and delete them. You just cannot add new photos beyond the 200 MiB limit or new Moments of the Day, and custom colours stop being applied.

Preparations already in progress can still be ticked off, edited and closed after Pro ends, and yearbook PDFs you have saved stay yours. New preparations, scenario edits, the Family week, shopping and meal suggestions and new yearbook exports need Rodinka Pro.

Availability and sales channels

If purchases are enabled, the web uses Paddle as seller (Merchant of Record), Android uses Google Play and iOS uses Apple’s App Store. BMC is for existing subscribers only. The Terms of Use explain each channel’s contractual roles and procedures.

Renewal and cancellation

Manage an existing subscription through the original purchasing channel and account. Turning off renewal ordinarily preserves Pro until the end of the paid period; deleting the account or uninstalling the app does not stop payments. Withdrawal, defect claims and refunds are separate rights.
